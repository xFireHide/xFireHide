## Try my projects

Three tools you can open directly in your browser.

| Project | What it does | Open the app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **toSVG** | Turn raster images into SVG vectors. Image processing runs locally in your browser. | [firesvg.netlify.app](https://firesvg.netlify.app/) |
| **FireConverter** | Convert images, audio, video, and PDFs in your browser without uploading your files. | [fireconverter.netlify.app](https://fireconverter.netlify.app/) |

## Behind the projects

- **Browser tools:** local processing, WebAssembly, and downloadable results.
- **Web interfaces:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
- **Focus:** useful features, clear interfaces, and an honest explanation of what each tool can do.
